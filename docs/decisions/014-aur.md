# AUR

**Work in progress. Nothing here is decided yet.**

This document will own whether an OpinionatedArch machine reaches the AUR at all and, if it does, with which helper, how that helper is installed, and under which account packages are built.

[Remaining](../state/001-remaining.md) carries it as a pending decision and names `paru` as the candidate. The build account is the part that reaches outside this document: [Work Contexts and Accounts](000-work-contexts-and-accounts.md) leaves room for accounts that exist for something other than an area of the operator's activity, and a build user is one of those, so its name, its home and what it is allowed to do come from here.

[Remaining](../state/001-remaining.md) also carries `oparch-aur` as a tool with no specification — a browser across the AUR with a PKGBUILD inspector — which builds packages the way this document decides rather than deciding it itself.
